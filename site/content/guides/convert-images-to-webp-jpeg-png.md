Image Scraper Pro can prepare images while it downloads them: convert the format, shrink large photos, fit them to exact social-media sizes and keep each file under a size limit. Everything runs on your device, and no image is uploaded anywhere.

## Convert the format

In the dashboard's bottom bar, choose **Convert**: Original, PNG, JPEG or WebP (with adjustable quality). Every **Download** and **Download ZIP** then uses that format.

- **WebP** is usually much smaller than JPEG or PNG at similar quality, which makes it ideal for websites.
- **JPEG** works everywhere and suits photos.
- **PNG** is lossless and keeps transparency.

> **Tip:** Not sure WebP is worth it? Open the **Audit** window and click **Measure WebP savings**. It converts your JPEG and PNG images and shows the real difference in size.

## Resize while downloading

In the options, set **Resize: max width** and **Resize: max height** in pixels. Images are scaled down to fit, keeping their shape. They're never enlarged, and GIFs and SVGs are saved unchanged unless you pick a conversion.

## Exact sizes, file size limits and watermarks

Click **Prepare…** in the dashboard to apply more to the whole selection:

- **Exact sizes:** presets for social posts and link previews, or a custom width and height, either cropped to fill or fitted on a background colour.
- **Keep each file under** a size limit: quality is lowered first, then the dimensions, until each file fits.
- **Watermark:** your own text or logo, with position, opacity and size, and a preview before you download.

## Edit a single image

Open an image in the preview and click **Edit…** to rotate, flip, crop by dragging, or resize it. The edit runs at full resolution. Download the result, or copy it to the clipboard as PNG, JPEG or WebP.

## Save as PDF or a contact sheet

From **Export ▾**, choose **PDF document** for one image per page (sized to each image, or fitted onto A4 or Letter), or **Contact sheet** for a PNG grid of the selected images with their names and sizes. Handy for sharing a selection with a client.
