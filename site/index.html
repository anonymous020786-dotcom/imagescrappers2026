FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is Image Scraper Pro free?

Yes. It is free and open source under the GPL-3.0 licence, with no account, no ads and no paid tier.

Does the extension collect my data?

No. Everything — scanning, editing, metadata removal, the SEO audit, PDFs and collections — runs in your browser. The extension has no analytics or tracking and sends nothing to the developer. Only reverse image search, when you choose it, opens the search engine you pick. Read the privacy policy.

Which browsers are supported?

Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Brave, Opera, Vivaldi, Firefox (desktop and Android, version 121+) and Safari 16.4+ on macOS, all built from the same Manifest V3 code.

How do I install it from the ZIP file?

Unzip the download, open chrome://extensions (or edge://extensions), turn on Developer mode, click Load unpacked and choose the unzipped folder. In Firefox, open about:debugging, choose This Firefox and click Load Temporary Add-on. The install guide covers every browser.

What does the image SEO audit check?

Missing, generic, keyword-stuffed, overly long or reused alt text; non-descriptive file names; missing or undersized Open Graph and Twitter images; missing structured-data images; oversized files; images that could be WebP; and lazy-loading mistakes. It exports a CSV report and a Google image sitemap.

Can it download images from a whole website?

Yes. The bulk scraper accepts thousands of URLs, and the crawler follows links, pagination and sitemap.xml with depth, scope and speed controls. It respects robots.txt by default.

Am I allowed to use the images I download?

That depends on each image's licence and the website's terms. The extension shows the creator, credit and licence a page or file states, and records each image's source in ZIP downloads, but it is not legal advice. Please respect copyright.
